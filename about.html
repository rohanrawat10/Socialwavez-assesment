Our Story

From a Laptop to a Leading Studio

SW Technologies was founded in 2021 by a small group of web developers who were tired of seeing businesses struggle with slow, outdated websites. We believed every company — no matter its size — deserved a world-class online presence.

What started as a two-person freelance operation quickly grew into a full-service web development studio, trusted by 30+ clients across India and beyond. We've delivered e-commerce platforms, corporate websites, portfolio sites, and SaaS landing pages that have driven real, measurable business outcomes.

Today, we continue to grow — but our mission stays the same: build fast, modern, and purposeful websites that make our clients' competitors nervous.
